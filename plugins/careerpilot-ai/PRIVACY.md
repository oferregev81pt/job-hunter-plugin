# Privacy Policy — careerpilot-ai

_Last updated: 2026-10-13_

**careerpilot-ai** is a set of skills (instruction files) that run inside Claude. The plugin itself is not a service: it has no server, no account system, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The author does not receive, store or have access to any data you enter or any files the skills create.

## What happens to your data

- **Conversation content** (your CV, job descriptions, answers to interview questions) is processed by Claude, under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you use (Claude Code, Claude desktop or claude.ai). See [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Local files:** some skills save files on your own machine, such as your CV at `./job-search-tracker/cv.md` and tracker files. They stay where you put them; delete them any time.
- **Notion (optional):** if you choose to use the `notion-connector` or `target-company-finder` skills with Notion, data is written to your own Notion workspace through the connector you authorized, and is governed by [Notion's privacy policy](https://www.notion.so/notion/Privacy-Policy-3468d120cf614d4c9014c09f6adc9091).
- **Web research:** skills such as `company-researcher` may use Claude's web search to look up public information about companies. Search queries are handled by Claude's tools, not by this plugin.

## Third parties

The plugin does not send data to any third party on its own. Any data flow comes from the Claude product and the connectors (such as Notion) that you enable.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will be committed to this repository with a new date. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/oferregev81pt/job-hunter-plugin/issues.

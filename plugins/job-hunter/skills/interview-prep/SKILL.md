# Interview Prep

Generate a prep document tailored to the specific interview stage the user is in.

## Instructions

Ask inputs one at a time:

1. "Which stage are you prepping for?"
   Options: "Applying now — I need an application package" / "HR / recruiter screen" / "Professional / hiring manager interview" / "Home assignment"
   (Wait for response.)

2. "Please paste your CV below." (Wait for response.)

3. "Paste the job description." (Wait for response.)

4. "Do you have any company research or intel to share? Paste it here, or say 'none'." (Wait for response.)

5. Generate the appropriate document based on the stage chosen:
